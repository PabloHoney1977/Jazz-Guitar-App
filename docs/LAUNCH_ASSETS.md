# Launch-day assets

Turnkey copy + a demo-video plan for the public push. Pair these so the app
goes live and gets seen the same day (the "sit unnoticed" failure mode is
launching into silence).

---

## A. Demo video (the priority asset)

Goal: one **30-second vertical (1080×1920)** clip for TikTok / YouTube Shorts /
Instagram Reels. The app's superpower on video is that you **hear the band** —
walking bass + ride + comping under a pulsing chord grid. Lead with that.

You can reuse the same footage as an **App Store app preview** (Apple allows up
to 3, 15–30s each — see note D).

### Why DIY (and it's quick)
The clip is just a screen-recording of you using the app. Best source: run it on
a **real iPhone** (or the iOS Simulator), record with **iOS built-in Screen
Recording** (Control Center) — it captures the app's audio cleanly. Then trim +
caption in **CapCut** (free) or iMovie. ~30 min start to finish.

### Shot list / storyboard (no voiceover needed — captions + app audio)

| Time | On screen | Caption (big, top third) | Audio |
|------|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 0:00–0:03 | Play tab mid-loop, neck lighting the voicing, bar grid pulsing gold | **"Jazz guitar — with a band that never stops"** | App backing track (bass + ride + comp) |
| 0:03–0:08 | Bar grid cycling a ii–V–I, chord names visible | "Real changes. Walking bass. Ride cymbal." | keep the track playing |
| 0:08–0:13 | Keys tab — tap a chord, neck shows the voicing + guide-tone dots | "See every chord, in every key" | a chord strum |
| 0:13–0:18 | Guide tab — scroll the learning path / a stage card | "A path from first grip to first standard" | track back under |
| 0:18–0:23 | Ear Training — tap play, then the reveal flips to the answer | "Train the ear that ties it together" | the interval/chord sound |
| 0:23–0:30 | End card: icon + "Jazz Guitar Lab" + CTA | **"Free on the App Store · Pro once, no subscription"** | track resolves / button tap |

### Recording tips
- Set the app to **dark theme** (looks better on video) and a comfortable BPM (~80).
- In the Play tab, let a full ii–V–I cycle so viewers see the grid move *and*
  the neck change. Record a few extra seconds at the head/tail to trim cleanly.
- Capture each section as its own short take, then assemble — easier than one
  perfect run.
- Keep the app's own audio as the music bed. It's free, it's yours, and "you can
  hear it" is the differentiator. (If a platform flags audio, swap in
  royalty-free music from CapCut's library.)
- Burn the captions in large and high-contrast — most people watch muted at first.

## B. r/JazzGuitar launch post

**Read the subreddit rules first** — many guitar subs restrict self-promo to
certain days or require it be mostly-contribution, not a drop-link. Be
transparent it's yours, lead with value, invite hard feedback. Post from an
account with some history if you can.

**Title:**
> I built a free app for practicing ii–V–Is with a backing band — would love this community's honest feedback

**Body:**
> I've been learning jazz guitar and kept hitting the same wall: I'd drill
> voicings in silence and never actually *hear* them move through a progression.
> So I built the tool I wanted.
>
> **Jazz Guitar Lab** does a few things:
> - **Play-along** any ii–V–I (major/minor), jazz blues, tritone sub, secondary
>   dominants, or a few standards — with walking bass, ride, and guitar comping.
>   Smart voice leading picks the nearest inversions so you can see chords connect.
> - **Every chord in every key** with shells, Drop 2/3, and rootless voicings,
>   guide tones, and scale overlays on the neck.
> - **Ear training** — intervals, triads, 7th-chord qualities, cadences.
> - A **16-stage guided path** from the four chord qualities to playing a standard.
>
> It's free to start (shells, the major ii–V–I, core ear training, and the whole
> guide). There's a one-time $9.99 Pro unlock for the drop/rootless voicings, all
> the play forms, and full ear training — **one-time, no subscription**, because I
> hate subscriptions as much as you do.
>
> I'd really value brutal feedback from real players: does the comping feel right?
> What's missing? Where does the theory get it wrong? Link in the comments / below.
>
> [link]

**Tone notes:** humble, specific, ask-for-feedback (not "check out my app").
Reply to every comment for the first 48h — engagement signals help the post and
builds goodwill.

## D. App Store app preview video (optional, reuse the footage)
- Apple allows up to 3 preview videos per device size, **15–30s**, recorded from
  the app (no marketing-only footage / no hands).
- Easiest: trim your social clip to a 15–25s App-Store-clean version (drop any
  external captions Apple might reject; show the app doing things).
- Captured at the right device resolution it uploads straight to App Store Connect.
- A preview video measurably lifts product-page → download conversion.
