Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Full purchase on a new device?
Open the app, tap ··· in the top-right corner, then tap Restore Purchase. Your unlock will be restored automatically from the App Store.
What's included in the free Essentials tier?
Shell voicings, major II–V–I play-along, melodic interval ear training (5 consonant intervals), first 4 chord types in Any Chord, and all 16 Guide stages. No time limit — Essentials is free forever.
What does Full unlock?
Drop 2, Drop 3, and Rootless voicings; all play forms (minor II–V–I, jazz blues, tritone sub, secondary dominants, custom); all 12 ear training intervals plus harmonic mode, triads, 7th chords, and cadences; and all extended chord types (9ths, 11ths, 13ths, altered). One-time $9.99.
The audio sounds choppy or doesn't play.
Make sure your device is not on silent/ring switch muted. On first launch the app downloads guitar samples — give it a moment on a good connection. Closing other audio apps can also help.
I have a feature request.
Use the contact form above and select "Feature request" — we read every one.
