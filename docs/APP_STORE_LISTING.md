## Screenshots (the real conversion driver)

People decide from screenshots before reading. Sell the **outcome**, not the UI.
Required sizes: 6.7", 6.5" iPhone, 12.9" iPad. Add a one-line caption on each.

Suggested 5-shot story (capture from the running app):
1. **Play tab mid-loop** — caption "Play real changes with a band."
2. **Keys tab, a chord with guide tones + scale overlay** — "See every chord in every key."
3. **Guide path** — "A step-by-step path from first grip to first standard."
4. **Ear training reveal** — "Train the ear that turns shapes into music."
5. **Chords/Any-Chord with a Drop 2 voicing** — "Shells, Drop 2, Drop 3, rootless."

Tip: frame on a real device mockup with the caption baked in; plain raw
screenshots convert worse.

## Launch / discoverability plan (in priority order)

1. **ASO first (free).** Nail subtitle + keywords + screenshots. This is the
   compounding, no-cost channel. Target what jazz guitarists actually type:
   "jazz guitar chords", "ii V I", "jazz comping", "guitar ear training".
2. **Small paid-ad test ($150–300).** Apple Search Ads is the highest-intent
   channel — bid on your own keywords and adjacent terms. Goal of the test is to
   learn **cost-per-install** and **install→IAP conversion** *before* spending
   real money. If a $9.99 one-time can't cover CPI at small scale, fix the
   funnel before scaling.
3. **Community (slow, compounding).** r/JazzGuitar, jazz-guitar Discords/forums,
   a couple of 20-second YouTube/TikTok clips of the Play tab in action. Niche
   communities convert far better than broad reach for a tool like this.

## Honest expectations
A niche one-time-purchase app lives or dies on (a) ranking for a searched term
and/or (b) a repeatable paid funnel with positive unit economics. Treat the
$150–300 test as the real go/no-go for paid acquisition. Passive income here
means steady installs × a healthy install→purchase rate — both earned, neither
automatic.
